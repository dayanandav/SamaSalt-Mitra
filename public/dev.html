Data import

Developer tool. Load the latest official Jan Aushadhi (PMBJP) product list and MRPs from PMBI, check what changes, then apply it to the live database.

Access

Admin token

Step 1

Download the latest list from PMBI

  1. Open the official PMBI product MRP list.
  2. Use the page's Export / CSV download, which saves every product with its MRP (columns: Sr No, Drug Code, Generic Name, Unit Size, MRP, Group Name).
  3. Note today's date: it becomes the "list date" shown under every Jan Aushadhi price in the app.

Step 2

Preview the changes

Step 3

Apply to the live database

Replaces the whole Jan Aushadhi product table in one step (all or nothing) and sets the list date. Brand data and Kendras are not touched.
